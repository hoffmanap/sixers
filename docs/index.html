2. Who actually has the ball

Four players have a real claim to touches; there's one ball. Pick-and-roll ball-handler frequency is the most direct trace of who's initiating, season over season. Click a player to isolate their line.

PnR ball-handler poss. % · click to isolate

Embiid's PRBH share is ~0% every season measured — he's never been a pick-and-roll initiator. LeBron's has fallen from 20.8% to 11.7% since 2022-23.

3. Maxey's speed, Embiid's mobility

Real on-court net rating for the Maxey-Embiid pairing, season by season, next to Maxey's distance-run tracking. Does Maxey's downhill pace get unlocked or capped by Embiid sharing the floor?

Maxey+Embiid net rating (bars) · Maxey distance run (line)

Net rating collapsed to -3.2 in 2024-25 — the exact season Embiid's health limited him to 315 shot attempts all year — and recovered to +7.2 this season. Looks like an availability problem, not a pace mismatch: among every 600+ minute duo on the 2025-26 team, Maxey+Embiid actually posts the best net rating.
